Tutor-M: the market says you're worth more.

What the prices of 8,952 Preply English tutors say about one tutor's rate — September 2026
Tutor-M has taught more lessons than 84% of all tutors on the platform — yet her $20 is what US native tutors with fewer than ten reviews charge. Tutors with her track record charge $29. That gap is worth about $12,000 a year gross (≈ $9,700 after Preply's commission) at her current teaching pace.

What raising the price is worth

A structural point in Tutor-M's favor: she is at Preply's best commission tier. Preply takes a sliding commission — 33% for new tutors, falling to an 18% floor once a tutor passes 400 teaching hours. With ~2,300 hours taught, Tutor-M pays the minimum 18%, so every dollar of a raise is worth 82 cents to her — while a new tutor undercutting her keeps only 67 cents. And since Preply keeps 100% of every trial lesson, each new student starts as a free lesson; the regular-lesson price has to carry the whole relationship.

Why it's safe to raise

To be fair about what this chart can and can't say: it doesn't prove that raising prices makes students stay — more likely, tutors who are worth more both charge more and keep students longer. But that's exactly the group Tutor-M's track record puts her in, and students who've stayed for years aren't staying because of the $20.

The 5.0 rating is safe. Nearly nine out of ten tutors show 4.9+ stars, so a rare imperfect rating changes nothing. What actually matters is the number of reviews.

The one gap: not asking for reviews

Reviews are the platform's real currency — they're what justify each price step. Tutor-M has taught 2,809 lessons but banked only 23 reviews. The fix costs nothing: ask after every milestone (“you passed your interview — would you leave a review?”). At 50 reviews, the market comfortably supports $29–30.
